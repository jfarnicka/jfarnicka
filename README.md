#### Work

**[Heartform](https://jxa77a111.github.io/heartform/)** · six real hearts, each analysed over five minutes and two ECG leads, turned into a luminous 3D form and a granular drone built from its own signal.
`Python` `NeuroKit2` `signal processing` `Web Audio` `Canvas` · [code](https://github.com/jxa77a111/heartform)

**[Heart Atelier](https://jxa77a111.github.io/heart-atelier/)** · an ECG lab: beat detection checked against cardiologists' labels, rhythm analysis, and a reader that turns photos of paper ECGs back into signals.
`JavaScript` `Pan–Tompkins` `template matching` `image processing` · [code](https://github.com/jxa77a111/heart-atelier)

**[Glyph Atelier](https://jxa77a111.github.io/glyph-atelier/)** · paintings transcribed into glyphs, halftone and brush strokes, with a live mode that reacts to music.
`Canvas` `Sobel filter` `FFT` `Web Audio` · [code](https://github.com/jxa77a111/glyph-atelier)

**[Gedächtnispalast](https://jxa77a111.github.io/mindpalace-med-mnemotechnics/)** · a mind-palace study tool for the German medical exam, with AI-assisted mnemonics and spaced repetition.
`JavaScript` `LLM API` `spaced repetition` · [code](https://github.com/jxa77a111/mindpalace-med-mnemotechnics)
